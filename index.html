04 / The fine print
Privacy Policy +

MJournal is designed to keep your reflections yours. Your mood entries and notes are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information or require an account to use the app.

When you choose to download MJournal, your browser handles the download. You can delete your entries or the app at any time.

Terms of Service +

By using MJournal, you agree to use it for personal reflection and to keep your device and downloaded files secure. MJournal is a journaling tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or a replacement for professional care.
