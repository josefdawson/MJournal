04 / The fine print
Privacy Policy +

MJournal keeps your reflections yours. Your account and your mood entries, notes, and saved memories are stored locally on your device, and your password is only ever kept as a salted hash. We do not sell your personal information.

If you use the optional journal chat, the entry and messages you send are shared with our AI provider so it can generate replies. MJournal does not retain those conversations on its servers. You can delete your entries, or delete your account and all of its data, at any time from the app's Settings.

Terms of Service +

By using MJournal, you agree to use it for personal reflection and to keep your account credentials and device secure. MJournal is a journaling tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, or a replacement for professional care.

The app is provided as-is by Little Dev. We may improve or update the app over time.
